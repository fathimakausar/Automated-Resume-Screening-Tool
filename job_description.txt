Job Title: Junior Data Analyst (Python)

About the role:
We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to clean, analyse and visualise business data and to
automate recurring reports using Python. You will work with the sales and operations teams to
turn raw data into clear dashboards and insights.

Responsibilities:
- Write SQL queries to extract and combine data from databases
- Clean and analyse datasets using Python, pandas and NumPy
- Build reports and data visualization dashboards in Excel, Power BI or Tableau
- Apply basic statistics to find trends and explain results to non-technical teams
- Use Git for version control and document your work

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or a similar role
- Strong knowledge of Python, pandas, SQL and Excel
- Good communication skills

Nice to have: Power BI, Tableau, matplotlib, statistics, Git
